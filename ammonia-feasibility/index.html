Haber-Bosch loop performance

The synthesis loop is modelled with the Temkin-Pyzhev rate equation and Peng-Robinson VLE for the NH3-H2-N2 mixture at 150 bar. The per-pass conversion is limited to 18% to avoid excessive recycle compression cost. Recycle compressor power is 0.22 MWh/t NH3. The loop achieves an ammonia concentration of 20 mol% at the converter outlet and 98.5% overall carbon-free nitrogen conversion.

Storage and shipping

Ammonia is stored as refrigerated liquid at -33 °C. For 3 days of production, two 3,000 m³ spheres are required. The shipping cost to Northwest Europe is estimated at €18/t NH3 for a 30,000 t vessel on a Hamburg–Rotterdam route, including boil-off loss of 0.1%/day. The delivered cost in Rotterdam is therefore approximately €630/t NH3.
