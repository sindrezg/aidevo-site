What Pre-FEED means

Pre-FEED (pre-front-end engineering design) is the study that turns a project concept into a sufficiently defined package for a full FEED or EPC tender. It sits between early feasibility and detailed engineering. The goal is not final design — it is to bound the decision: technology, capacity, site, major equipment, energy and mass balance, emissions, schedule, risk and an order-of-magnitude cost.

Aidevo Pre-FEED methodology

Aidevo Pre-FEED runs on physics-based feasibility engines, not spreadsheet estimates. Every mass and energy balance is deterministic. CAPEX is built from equipment sizing against vendor-calibrated cost curves. OPEX uses real utility tariffs and operating assumptions. The output is reviewed by Aidevo engineers and delivered as a signed report with an interactive 3D process twin.

Uncertainty and sensitivity

A typical Pre-FEED CAPEX estimate has ±30% accuracy at AACE Class 4. The largest uncertainties are usually: equipment cost escalation (±15–20%), site conditions (±10–15%), feedstock quality (±10–20%), and regulatory requirements (±10–25%). Aidevo includes 10,000-run Monte Carlo sensitivity on every major economic output.
