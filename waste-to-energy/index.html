ENGINEERING SCOPE

From initial screening to defined Pre-FEED.

The scope and deliverables are agreed for each project. Here is how a typical engagement progresses.

Site screening

Feedstock characterisation, technology shortlisting, site-level energy balance and a directional economic read. Identifies whether to invest in a full study.

Feasibility study

Detailed mass and energy balance, technology selection with sensitivity analysis, emissions profile, CAPEX/OPEX estimate, gate fee and revenue scenarios. A signed verdict with documented assumptions.

Pre-FEED package

Process flow diagrams, equipment lists and datasheets, utility requirements, battery limits, preliminary P&IDs, cost model and a handover package for FEED.

TECHNOLOGY COVERAGE

Waste-to-energy and waste-to-methanol.

The process model covers the main commercial and emerging conversion routes. Technology selection is based on feedstock properties, scale and project requirements.

Waste-to-energy

Grate combustion, fluidised bed, rotary kiln, gasification with gas engine or combined cycle. Heat recovery steam generation, district heating integration, CCS bolt-on assessment.

Waste-to-methanol

Gasification with syngas cleanup and methanol synthesis. Feedstock flexibility analysis, gas conditioning requirements, catalyst performance under varying compositions. Biomethanol certification pathways.

Economic sensitivity (illustrative ranges)

Gate fee required for breakeven depends on CAPEX, electricity offtake price and heat revenue. For projects at this scale, published industry data suggests CAPEX in the range of 8,000–12,000 EUR per installed kWe for grate systems. Actual economics depend on site-specific factors including grid connection, waste supply security, permitting timeline and financing structure. A feasibility study quantifies these sensitivities for the specific project configuration.

This example uses published reference data and standard engineering correlations. It does not represent a recommendation, forecast or guarantee of project outcomes. Actual feasibility study results depend on site-specific data and validated assumptions.
