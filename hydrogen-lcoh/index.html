Compression and storage

Compression from 30 bar electrolyser outlet to 700 bar dispensing pressure adds approximately 2.1 kWh/kg H₂. Aidevo models this as a 3-stage compression train with intercooling, achieving 65% overall isentropic efficiency. Storage is sized for 2 days of production (≈22 t H₂) in Type-3 tube trailers. Combined compression + storage contributes €0.43/kg to LCOH.

Subsidies and offtake

In the Hamburg case, the project is eligible for German Klimaschutzvertrag (KSV) hydrogen offtake support and H2Global double-auction support. Aidevo's model includes these instruments as scenario inputs. With full subsidy stack, effective LCOH to the offtaker falls to approximately €4.50/kg H₂.
