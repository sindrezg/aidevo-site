The base case uses a moving-grate incineration line with dry flue-gas cleaning, SNCR/SCR, activated carbon injection and baghouse filtration. The plant processes 412,000 t/y of residual MSW, produces 30 MW net electricity and supplies 12 MW of district heat to a nearby industrial cluster. CAPEX includes EPC, civil works, grid connection, cooling water intake and 6 months commissioning support. The project meets EU Industrial Emissions Directive limits with margin.

Electrical efficiency and energy balance

The base-case moving-grate plant achieves a gross electrical efficiency of 24.8% and a net efficiency of 22.5% after parasitic loads. Total energy input from waste LHV is 1,280 GWh/y. Electricity export is 240 GWh/y and heat export is 96 GWh/y, giving a total useful energy output of 336 GWh/y and an R1 energy recovery index of 0.845.

Uncertainty and sensitivity

CAPEX uncertainty is estimated at ±18% (AACE Class 4). A 10,000-run Monte Carlo simulation gives the following impact on gate fee:
